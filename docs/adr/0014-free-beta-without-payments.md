# Free beta without payments

## Context

Doni needs real users to validate that existing holders want it before investing in monetisation. Payment collection in the UEMOA (mobile money, cards) adds integration, tax and compliance work, and charging users would make the data licence question (ADR-0003) more pressing.

## Decision

The MVP is a **free beta with no payments**: no subscriptions, no paywalled features, no payment integration, and no paid placement (ADR-0002). Monetisation is decided after the beta and after the data licence terms are known.

## Rejected alternatives

- **Freemium from launch**: requires payment integration and deciding which features are premium before we know what users value.
- **Paid SGI placement or referral fees**: conflicts with the information-only stance.
- **Advertising**: harms trust in a financial tool and adds tracking.

## Consequences

- No billing code, tables or provider in the MVP.
- The data licence terms we negotiate must cover a later commercial model, or be renegotiated.
