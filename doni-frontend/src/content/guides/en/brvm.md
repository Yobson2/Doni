## One exchange for eight countries

The **BRVM** (Bourse Régionale des Valeurs Mobilières) is the stock exchange shared by the eight countries of the West African Economic and Monetary Union (UEMOA): Benin, Burkina Faso, Côte d'Ivoire, Guinea-Bissau, Mali, Niger, Senegal and Togo. It is headquartered in Abidjan.

Mainly **shares** of listed companies and **bonds** trade on it. Amounts are in West African CFA francs (XOF).

## Who does what

- **The BRVM** runs the trading sessions and publishes prices.
- **The AMF-UMOA** (Autorité des Marchés Financiers de l'UMOA) regulates the regional financial market. It licenses intermediaries.
- **SGIs** (Sociétés de Gestion et d'Intermédiation) are the licensed intermediaries through which an individual buys and sells securities.
- **The Dépositaire Central / Banque de Règlement (DC/BR)** holds securities centrally and settles trades.

## The Bulletin Officiel de la Cote

After each session, the BRVM publishes the **Bulletin Officiel de la Cote (BOC)**. For each security it gives the day's closing price and traded volume, and it gives the value of the indices, including the **BRVM Composite**.

Doni shows these **closing prices**, always with the date of the session they apply to. They are not real-time prices.
