On the BRVM, individuals do not place orders themselves: they go through an **SGI** (Société de Gestion et d'Intermédiation), an intermediary licensed by the AMF-UMOA. This guide describes the usual steps. The details (documents required, minimum amounts, fees) vary from one SGI to another: ask the SGI directly.

## 1. Choose an SGI

Check that the SGI is on the list of licensed intermediaries published by the AMF-UMOA. Compare what matters to you: fees, how you can place an order (branch, phone, app), the statements you receive.

Doni's [SGI directory](/sgi) lists them in alphabetical order, with their fees where they are published, each with its source and date. Doni does not rank SGIs and is not paid by any of them.

## 2. Open a securities account

The SGI opens a **securities account** in your name (where your shares are recorded) and a linked **cash account**. It usually asks for an identity document, proof of address and a signed account agreement. Read the fee schedule that comes with it.

## 3. Fund the account

You pay into the cash account the amount you intend to invest, fees included, using the payment methods the SGI accepts.

## 4. Place an order

You tell the SGI the security, the side (buy), the quantity and the order type. With a **limit order**, you set the highest price you are willing to pay. The order is filled during a session if a seller is found at that price, sometimes in several parts, sometimes not at all.

## 5. Check the trade confirmation

Once the order is filled, the SGI sends you a **trade confirmation** (avis d'opéré): date, quantity, price, fees. Keep it: it is the record of your purchase.

## 6. Track your position in Doni

In Doni, record the purchase as it appears on the trade confirmation: date, quantity, price per share, fees and SGI. Doni works out your **PMP** (weighted average purchase price, fees included) and the value of your position at the latest published closing price.
