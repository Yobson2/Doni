When a listed company decides to pay a dividend, it is announced in a **notice** published by the BRVM. Here is what the notice contains and how to read it.

## What the notice states

- **The gross amount per share**: what the company pays for each share, before tax.
- **The net amount per share**: what is left after the tax on income from securities (IRVM) is withheld. The rate depends in particular on the company's country.
- **The ex-date** (date de détachement): from this day on, the share trades without the right to the dividend.
- **The payment date**: the day the dividend is paid.

## Who receives the dividend

You are entitled to it for the shares you held **before the ex-date**. A share bought on or after that date does not carry this dividend.

On the ex-date, the share price often falls by an amount close to the dividend: this is not a loss, the value is paid out separately.

## What you actually receive

On the payment date, the SGI credits your cash account. The amount received can differ from "net amount per share × number of shares": rounding, any SGI fees. Your account statement is the reference.

## In Doni

Doni keeps apart the dividend the company announced (reference dividend) and the amount you confirm you received (user dividend). Only the latter enters your history and your return. So enter the net amount shown on your statement.
