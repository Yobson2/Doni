## What we store

**Your account**

- Your email address and your password. The password is never stored as such: only a one-way hash of it is kept.
- Your first and last name, if you give them.
- Your role, whether the account is active, your display currency (XOF or EUR), and when the account was created and last updated.

**Your sessions**

- While you are signed in, a session token is kept in a cookie restricted to the sign-in service. On our side we only keep a hash of it, with its expiry date and whether it has been revoked.

**Your portfolios**

- The name of each portfolio you create.
- Each entry you record: the security, the date, the quantity, the price, the fees and the SGI the trade went through; opening positions and user dividends; the corrections you make (an edited or deleted entry is kept as voided, with what replaced it).

**Your watchlist**

- The securities you follow and when you added them.

**In your browser**

- Your language, theme and font choices and whether the side menu is open, kept on your device only.

Market data (prices, companies, corporate actions) is public data and is not about you.

## Your rights

- **Download your data**: Settings, Account, export. You receive one file with your account, your sessions (without any secret), your portfolios with their full history, and your watchlist.
- **Delete your account**: Settings, Account, delete, confirmed with your password. Your account and all the data above are deleted from the live database at once. They remain in backups until those backups expire.
