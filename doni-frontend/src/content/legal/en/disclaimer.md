## Information and education only

Doni provides information and education only. **Nothing on Doni is investment advice**, a recommendation to buy, sell or hold a security, or a solicitation.

## What Doni does

- Doni shows data published by the BRVM, mainly the closing prices of the Bulletin Officiel de la Cote, each with its date and source.
- Doni computes figures (value, PMP, gains, return) from the information you enter yourself.
- Doni publishes neutral guides and an SGI directory listed in alphabetical order.

## What Doni does not do

- Doni does not recommend any security and does not rate any company.
- Doni does not receive or route stock market orders and does not hold money.
- Doni is not paid by any SGI and sells no placement in the directory.
